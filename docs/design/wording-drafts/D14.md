## Draft

The `spendlimit` push goes out once a UTC day, when a turn first meets Anthropic's monthly limit that day. While the limit holds, the next day's first probe that meets it pushes again. Its priority is `urgent`, as for every limit alert.

| Part | Text |
|---|---|
| Title | Chat paused at Anthropic's monthly limit for October |
| Line | Raise the limit in the Anthropic Console, then in src/ops/limits.ts. The chat tries again within an hour. |

- The title says what happened and which month. The month's name comes from the UTC day of the turn that met the limit.
- The title names no amount. The limit that Anthropic enforces is the one in the Console. `src/ops/limits.ts` only copies it, so an amount from there can be wrong after Peter raises the limit in the Console and before he changes the file.
- The line names the next steps in the runbook's order: the Console first, where the change takes effect at once, then `src/ops/limits.ts`, which the `spend75` alert measures the month against. A change to that file is a release.
- "The chat tries again within an hour" tells Peter that he does not need to do anything more after he raises the limit. The next probe goes through at most an hour after the last turn that met the limit, when a visitor asks a question.
- The code that holds the text: `spendlimit()` in `src/ops/alerts.ts`.
