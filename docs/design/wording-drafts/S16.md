# S16: the source page's kind line and title

Status: wording draft

- Task: [S16](../../../tasks/modules/site.md#s16-source-pages)
- Source: SPEC-site, Views, "Source view" (the deep dive's kind line, approved with the spec) and "Standalone pages"; SPEC-knowledge, "Rendered text" (an interview holds "Peter's own words"); boards "After a question, source open" and "Source from a citation"

## Draft

The code that holds the texts: `KIND_LINES` and `kindLine()` in `src/site/shell/SourceView.tsx`, and the title in `head()` of `src/site/routes/sources.$id.tsx`.

The kind line is three parts, each a sentence: the kind, the roles, the number of passages. SPEC-site gives one example, a deep dive about one role with five passages. The draft adds the forms that the example does not show.

| Part | Draft | New or approved |
|---|---|---|
| Kind, deep dive | "Deep dive, written from Peter’s answers and approved by him." | Approved: SPEC-site's example |
| Kind, interview | "Interview, in Peter’s own words and approved by him." | New: the deep dive's form, with SPEC-knowledge's words for an interview |
| Roles, one role | "FedNow, 2021 – 2024." | Approved: SPEC-site's example |
| Roles, two or more | "Highspot, 2024 – 2026; FedNow, 2021 – 2024." Each role is its `x-short` label and its years, in the source's own order, joined by a semicolon. | New |
| Roles, none | The part is left out: "Interview, in Peter’s own words and approved by him. 3 passages." | New |
| Passages | "5 passages." and, for one, "1 passage." | The plural is approved; the singular is new |
| Years | "2021 – 2024"; "2024 – Present" for a role with no end date; one year alone, "2016", for a role that starts and ends in the same year | The first form is row 137's; the other two are new |

The source page's title, which the browser tab and the share preview show:

| Text | Draft | New or approved |
|---|---|---|
| Title | "<source title> — <name>", for example "Cutting FedNow's initial bundle from over 15 MB to under 1 MB — Peter Kloss" | New. The resume page's title is "<name> — Resume"; the workspace's is the name alone |
| Description | The source's `summary`, which Peter approves with the source | No new text |
| Link | "Ask the chat about this", after the last passage, to `/?source=<id>` | The text is SPEC-site's; its place after the passages is new |
