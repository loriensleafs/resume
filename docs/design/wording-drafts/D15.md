Status: wording draft

- Source: SPEC-deploy, "Limit alerts" (the `spend75` row shows the form: a title and one line that names the next step), "The store fails" (answers go on, and `reserve()` allows every turn, so Anthropic's limit is the only cap) and "Runbooks" (`store-failed.md`: the Upstash console shows whether the budget or an outage caused it)

## Draft

The `storefail` push goes out when a store call fails. Each function instance pushes it at most once a UTC day, because its marker cannot live in the store that failed. Its priority is `urgent`, as for every limit alert.

| Part | Text |
|---|---|
| Title | Chat store failed: answers go on without the $8 daily cap |
| Line | Check the Upstash console for an outage or a spent budget. |

- The title says what happened and what it means for visitors. The chat keeps answering, but the daily cap cannot count the day's cost, so Anthropic's $20 monthly limit is the only cap.
- The line names the next step. It follows the runbook `store-failed.md` (task D36): the Upstash console shows whether an outage or the $1 budget caused the failure.
- "$8" comes from `dailyCap` in `src/ops/limits.ts`, so the title follows a new cap.
- On a day with an outage, Peter can get one push from each function instance that saw a failure. A failed push is tried again by the first store failure 10 minutes later.
- The function log holds an `ops_error` line for each failed step. The line names the step (`reserve`, `take_back`, `record`, `append`, `alert_claim` or `alert_keep`) and one code, such as `HTTP_500`, `TIMEOUT` or `NETWORK`.
- The code that holds the text: `storefail()` in `src/ops/alerts.ts`.
