### The apostrophe

"Brigham and Women’s" takes the curly apostrophe (’, U+2019), not the straight one (').

- SPEC-content, Code Style, asks for curly apostrophes in content. `x-short` is content in `resume.yaml`.
- The role's `name` already uses it: "Brigham and Women’s Hospital".
- The board source writes the label with U+2019.
- SPEC-site, Labels, writes "Brigham and Women's" with a straight apostrophe. T1 leaves SPEC-site as it is; its line can take the curly form once Peter approves this draft.
