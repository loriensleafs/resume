### What differs from the boards

- The boards name a source by a short name: "The FedNow bundle cut: What Peter did" in the citations card, and "Source: the FedNow bundle cut" in the device matches. No field of an approved source holds a short name, so a passage's label takes the source's title, as SPEC-chat says. The title of `fednow-bundle-size` has 61 characters (see the C7 row in [build decisions](../build-decisions.md)).
- The board "Typing" labels a highlight's match "Resume: FedNow". The device index gives each resume entry its own label, so that highlight's label is "Resume: FedNow, highlight 2". C36 decides what a match shows.
