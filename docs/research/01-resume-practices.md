# Resume practices for a senior front-end and AI platform engineer

Findings from the October 2026 research pass that shaped the resume's content. That pass ran in an earlier session, and its source links were not kept. Re-check a claim against a current source before you rely on it for a new decision.

- **Headline wording.** In 2026 job postings, "AI Platform Engineer" usually means ML infrastructure (training and serving models). Peter's work is front-end platform and AI developer tooling, so the headline is "Front-End Platform Engineer, Design Systems and AI Developer Tooling".
- **Exact titles and dates.** Use the titles and dates HR records hold, because background checks (for example The Work Number) compare them. See [decision 0001](../decisions/0001-facts-from-records.md).
- **Length.** Two pages is normal at 15+ years of experience.
- **Layout.** One column is the safest layout for hiring software.
- **AI claims.** Back every AI claim with a concrete number or scope (for example "~100 people", "21 specs and 189 requirement documents").
