# The chat also answers about Peter's career story, working style, opinions, side projects and skill depth

Peter wants the chat to answer from "a comprehensive knowledge base about who I am", not only from the resume and nine project deep dives. On 2026-10-27 he chose six kinds of work material for the long-form interviews: his career story, his working style and leadership, stories from his work, his opinions about his field (such as design systems and AI adoption), his side projects, and how deep each skill goes and where he used it.

Decided 2026-10-27 by Peter. This widens [decision 0009](0009-chat-scope.md), which had left side projects out; the rest of 0009 stands.

Considered: interests outside work. Peter left them out, so the chat still refuses personal-life questions, along with salary, immigration, health and age.

Consequences:

- Each kind becomes interview sources in Peter's words ([decision 0016](0016-two-layer-knowledge.md)), approved by him before the chat can read them.
- Side projects are described with links only to public repositories.
- Opinions are stated as Peter's views, in his words, not as facts.
