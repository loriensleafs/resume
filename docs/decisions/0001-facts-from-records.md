# Every fact comes from Peter or his records

Titles and dates must match what an employer's HR records say, because a background check (for example The Work Number) compares them. So every fact in the resume source comes from Peter or from his own records, and nothing is rounded up, merged or upgraded.

Decided 2026-10 by Peter. He asked that job-history dates come from his email.

Evidence used:

- Highspot: the offer letter and Workday employment records give the title Sr. Software Development Engineer, December 2024 to August 2026.
- FedNow: Peter states his official title was Lead Front-End Engineer, after he was hired as Sr. UI Front End Developer. The resume shows years only.
- Education: Peter lists the University of Massachusetts Amherst with no degree and no year.
- Peter reviewed every highlight against what he did. For example, the resume states which efforts he led, and names the data table as the component he built.

Consequences: an agent that needs a fact asks Peter or reads his records. It never fills a gap with a plausible value. See the [edit-resume skill](../../.agents/skills/edit-resume/SKILL.md).
