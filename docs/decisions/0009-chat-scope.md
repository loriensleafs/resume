# Chat scope: resume facts plus approved logistics

The chat answers questions about Peter's work history, projects and skills, and the job-search logistics he approves (remote or on-site, location, availability, target roles), because recruiters ask those first. It refuses salary, immigration, health, age and personal-life questions, and anything the knowledge file does not cover, and points the visitor to Peter's email.

Decided 2026-10-26 by Peter.

Considered: resume facts only (cannot answer the first questions recruiters ask); also interests and side projects (more to approve and more room for off-topic answers).

Widened on 2026-10-27 by [decision 0019](0019-wider-chat-scope.md): the chat also answers about Peter's career story, working style, stories, opinions about his field, side projects and skill depth. Personal-life questions are still refused.
