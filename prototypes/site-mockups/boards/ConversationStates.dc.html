Conversation: messages and answer steps

Each column is drawn at the chat column’s width. Every message keeps a 28 px row under it for its actions, shown or not, and messages are 20 px apart, so the space between a question and its answer matches the space between an answer and the next question. Questions and answers use the same text size.

1. A question, hovered

Hovering a question shows when it was sent, then Retry, Edit and Copy as one group. Retry asks it again; Edit puts it back in the input; Copy copies its text. On an earlier question, Retry and Edit replace that answer and the ones after it. On phones, tapping a question shows the row.

3. Answer actions: an earlier answer hovered, the latest answer

Under each answer: Copy and Retry as one group, then when it was written. An earlier answer shows the row on hover. The latest answer always shows Copy and Retry; its time shows on hover. Retry asks the question again and replaces the answer. Each answer numbers its own citations from 1.
