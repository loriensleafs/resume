4. Answering, a question waiting

Enter during an answer queues the new question. It shows at the end of the conversation like any question, with a clock, and sends when the answer finishes. Send now stops the answer and sends it at once. Edit, or the Up arrow in an empty input, brings it back into the input; × removes it.
